A merchant, chama member, admin, or underwriter all start the same way: enter a phone number, get an OTP (logged to the console in this build, since no SMS provider is wired up yet), and verify it. A first-time merchant or chama member is prompted for their name and picks their own role at that point — underwriters and admins can't do this themselves; an admin has to create their account directly (bootstrapped for the very first admin via a one-off script), so that role never touches the public signup form. Once verified, the app hands back a short-lived access token and a rotating refresh token and drops the user onto the page for their role: merchants land on the Duka Ledger, chama members on the Chama Portal, underwriters on Underwriting, and admins on the Admin console.

From there the roles diverge. A merchant logs sales, expenses, and supplier payments into a hash-chained, database-enforced append-only ledger (each write is retry-safe via an idempotency key), manages inventory with automatic stock-level triggers, and can trigger a live credit-score evaluation that pulls their sales velocity, receivables, chama punctuality, and inventory margins through a trained, SHAP-explained model. Chama members (merchants can do this too) create or join savings groups, record contributions, and track on-time payment percentages. An underwriter works from a deliberately anonymized applicant list — score and risk tier only, no name or phone until a loan is actually approved — and approves loans without ever needing to know who the applicant is; the backend resolves that link internally. An admin's job is purely operational: onboard new underwriters, and manage every account's role, activity status, and access — deactivating a user immediately kills their sessions, not just their next login attempt. Everything list-shaped is paginated, and the whole system is built with the honest assumption that it's a working pilot, not a finished product — gaps like missing repayment UI or nav-only role checks on a couple of endpoints are documented rather than hidden.
